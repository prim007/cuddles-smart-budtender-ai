# Revise Privacy, Terms and POPIA Pages

## Goal
Create a clear legal area for Cuddles AI that reflects the services currently offered, gives users practical privacy choices, and makes the South African POPIA information easy to find.

## Changes
- Revise the existing Privacy Policy so it accurately covers accounts, AI chat and uploaded images, shop and delivery activity, newsletter consent through Brevo, analytics/cookies, business widgets, retention, international service providers, automated AI output, and user rights.
- Add a dedicated Terms and Conditions page covering eligibility, educational-only AI guidance, acceptable use, accounts, subscriptions and payments, partner shops and third-party services, user content, intellectual property, availability, termination, liability, and South African law.
- Add a dedicated POPIA page with the responsible party, lawful processing grounds, data-subject rights, direct-marketing consent and opt-out, cross-border processing, security safeguards, complaints, and Information Officer contact.
- Add all three pages to the public routes and footer, with links between related legal documents, the Trust page, Licensing terms, and DPA.
- Update the sitemap so search engines can discover the new legal pages.
- Keep the wording readable and clearly state that the documents are general legal information, not legal advice.

## Presentation
- Use the existing Cuddles sunset design, typography, buttons, cards, and footer.
- Make long legal content easier to scan with a compact contents menu, short sections, clear headings, and mobile-friendly spacing.
- Preserve the required cannabis education and not-medical-advice disclosures.

## Verification
- Check the Privacy, Terms, and POPIA pages on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm footer links, internal legal links, page titles/descriptions, and public routes work without signing in.
- Confirm the preview builds without errors.

## Important note
This improves transparency and coverage but is not a substitute for review by a South African attorney or registered Information Officer.
